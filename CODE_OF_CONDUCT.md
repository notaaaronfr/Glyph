# Code of Conduct

Glyph is built by people from many backgrounds and every level of experience. We want this to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Our standards

Do:

- be kind, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst
- critique ideas and code, never people
- accept feedback gracefully, and give it constructively
- credit others for their work

Don't:

- harass, insult or demean anyone, including through sexualised language, slurs or personal attacks
- troll, or derail discussions on purpose
- publish anyone's private information without their permission
- engage in any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project, as well as when representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@notaaaronfr](https://github.com/notaaaronfr), privately on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful to the community.
